The Utilization of LCHN Technology in Combination with pH Reactive PLGA Core Synthesis for the Treatment of Acute Myeloid Leukemia

I wrote this paper as the final project for BME405 Intro to Drug Delivery. The assignment was to choose a disease or affliction that could be improved by incorporating a drug delivery concept covered in the class. I chose to develop a nanoparticle delivery treatment for Acute Myeloid Leukemia. This paper is currently pending publication in the University of Tennessee, Knoxville's academic journal PURSUIT which is why only the title page and abstract are included.
